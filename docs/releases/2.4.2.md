2.4.2 is #1652's third part: a role's model can be a generator. An image or audio engine set up in
images.json or audio.json can be a role's model in place of a chat model (Settings: the role's
Generator row; the terminal: `wb route <role> --generator image:<engine>` or `audio:<engine>`).
Such a role makes files from a brief, with no agent: the orchestrator agent calls it with
`wb image --role <role>` or `wb audio --role <role>`, checks the candidates as it does any image or
clip, and commits the one it keeps. `wb start --role` on such a role says that instead of starting
a task. `none` gives the role its chat model back.
