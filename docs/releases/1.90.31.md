# Release checklist: 1.90.31

Filled in 2026-11-21 for `v1.90.31`, from `docs/release-checklist.md`. 1.90.31 closes seven
milestones at once, 1.90.25 to 1.90.31, which finished together. It has 82 fixes.

Of those, 62 come from the third readiness audit of 1.90.24 (#859–#926). The milestones split
them by area:
- the runner: handovers after a nudge, a dead server read as a slow model, cold loads mid-round;
- the task lifecycle and records: half-finished accepts, non-UTF-8 specs, batch acknowledgements;
- git: shallow clones, submodules in builds and the disk check, `git apply`;
- models, the GPU and platforms: remote boxes, `free_gpu`'s wait, WSL2 disk space;
- the router, tuning and the bench: chain costs, spec gaps on `next` and `accept`;
- the lane: dropped grants, toolchain homes, NSS sockets;
- the mod, the web chat and media: hand-back, notify polling, VAPID keys, cut-off replies.

The other 20 come from an outside readiness review (#930–#949). That review's #950, the macOS
nightly running past its limit, is fixed in part: the tests run in shards with a hang timer. It
moves to 1.90.32 until a nightly confirms it.

Two reviews of the fixes themselves found 23 regressions or incomplete fixes, all fixed here
before release. One was a race that CI hit about once in ten runs. A same-size rewrite in a
filled submodule, within the same second, went unseen by the edit check. The copied index got a
fresh mtime, which defeated git's racy-entry check; `gitops` now copies it with its times
(`shutil.copy2`).

Results are **pass**, **skip** (with why) or **fail** (with its issue).
