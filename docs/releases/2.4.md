# Release checklist: 2.4

Filled 2026-11-28 for `v2.4`, from `docs/release-checklist.md`; cut on a green `check` (the nightly
and the newest-OpenCode run started on it, not waited on).

2.4 is the first part of #1652, the role redesign. Every role, a built-in or one of the user's own,
now has a **Used for**: plain words the orchestrator agent reads to decide which role a piece of work
goes to. The built-ins come with theirs filled in, and the user can rewrite them. A new role can't be
added without one. **Works as** is gone from the Add a role form: a role no longer has to pretend to
be coding or writing. A role made before 2.4 keeps what it worked as. One with no Used for still
runs, but the orchestrator is told not to pick it until the user gives it one.

Every role also has **Skills**: Auto (the default, the orchestrator hands library skills to each
task as before) or Mine (only the skills the user gives the role, on every one of its tasks, and wb
refuses any the orchestrator tries to add). In Settings, each role's card has its Used for, its
Skills, and an upload for a SKILL.md of the user's own, which is checked and added to the skill
library like any other. In the terminal these are `wb route <role> --used-for`, `--skills` and
`--skill-from`.

The orchestrator now sees the roles with their Used for and Skills, also when every role runs on
the GPU (before, only once a role had an API model). Still to come in #1652: generator models
(image, sound, music, video) as a role's model, and the deep workflows' role cards merged into
these roles.

2.4 also fixes 2.3.3's port message: when one of a process's open files closed while wb was
looking, wb skipped that process, so a program of the user's own on the port could be called
another account's.

Results are **pass**, **skip** (with why) or **fail** (with its issue).
